### 2. Case Strength Scoring System

Every legal consultation now includes:

#### 🎯 **Strength Ratings:**
- **STRONG (70-95% win probability)**
  - Clear legal grounds
  - Solid evidence
  - Favorable precedents
  - Statutory protection

- **MODERATE (40-69% win probability)**
  - Valid legal claim
  - Some evidence gaps
  - Contested facts
  - Procedural challenges

- **WEAK (10-39% win probability)**
  - Limited evidence
  - Adverse precedents
  - Legal technicalities
  - Limitation issues

- **VERY WEAK (<10% win probability)**
  - Barred by limitation
  - No legal standing
  - Fundamental defects

## 💡 How It Works

### Automatic Case Strength Analysis

When a user provides detailed case information (30+ words with case details), the system automatically:

1. **Analyzes the case** using AI
2. **Rates case strength** (Strong/Moderate/Weak)
3. **Calculates win probability** (percentage)
4. **Identifies threshold factors** (favorable/adverse)
5. **Recommends strategy** with cost-benefit analysis

### Example Trigger Phrases:
- "My employer hasn't paid salary for 3 months. I have email proof..."
- "Landlord served eviction notice despite rent paid. I have all receipts..."
- "Purchased phone 2 months ago, defective, shop refuses refund. Have bill..."
