## 💬 Common Questions & Answers

### Q: "How is this different from Google?"
**A**: Google gives information. We provide professional legal analysis with case strength scoring and personalized action plans - like having a lawyer, not a search engine.

### Q: "How accurate is the AI?"
**A**: In demo mode, we use pattern matching with professional responses. With OpenAI, we use GPT-3.5-turbo trained on legal knowledge. All responses include disclaimers to consult lawyers for complex matters.

### Q: "Why use AI instead of real lawyers?"
**A**: AI provides instant, free preliminary guidance 24/7. For serious cases, we connect users with free legal aid centers. We complement, not replace, human lawyers.

### Q: "How do you make money?"
**A**: This is a social impact project. Future monetization: premium features, government grants, NGO partnerships, freemium model.

### Q: "What about data privacy?"
**A**: Sessions are anonymized, sensitive data is hashed, HTTPS encryption, compliance-ready architecture. No chat data is shared with third parties.

### Q: "Can it handle complex cases?"
**A**: For complex cases, AI provides preliminary analysis and directs users to appropriate legal professionals. It's designed for initial guidance, not final legal advice.
